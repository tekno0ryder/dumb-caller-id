# Privacy Policy — Dumb Caller ID (الكاشف الغبي)

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Dumb Caller ID shows a popup for incoming calls from unknown numbers, with a button to look the number up through a Telegram bot.

## What the app accesses

- **Incoming call numbers.** Android passes the app the number of incoming calls from numbers that are not in your contacts. The app never reads your contacts or your call log.
- **Call state.** The app checks when a call ends, only to close the popup.

## What is stored

The app keeps the last 10 incoming calls (time, number, and whether the popup was shown) on your phone, so you can look them up later. This list never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

## What is shared

A number is shared only when you tap **Look up**. The app then opens Telegram with the number filled in for the bot you chose in Settings. That bot is run by a third party, not by the developer of this app, and its own privacy policy applies.

**Copy diagnostics** in Settings copies a short report to your clipboard: your phone model, Android version, which setup steps are done, and the time and result of recent calls. It contains no phone numbers. It is created only when you tap the button, and leaves your phone only if you paste it somewhere yourself.

The app has no servers, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. It does not send any data to the developer.
